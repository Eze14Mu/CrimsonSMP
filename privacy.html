Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how CrimsonSMP ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our website, mobile/web applications, and gaming services.

1. Information We Collect

To provide user authentication and core server features via Google (Firebase OAuth), we collect the following information:

  • Google Account Data: Your display name, email address, and profile picture (provided when signing in via Google).
  • Minecraft Account Data: Your Minecraft username and UUID (Universally Unique Identifier).
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and connection logs to our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal data strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate you and grant access to your CrimsonSMP account.
  • To link your web application profile with your in-game Minecraft character.
  • To maintain server security and prevent unauthorized access or abuse.
  • To send essential account and security notifications.

3. Authentication & Third Parties (Firebase & Google)

We utilize Google OAuth managed through Firebase (Google Cloud Platform) to secure user logins. Your Google password is never shared with or stored on our servers. Processing of your account data is also subject to Google’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for commercial or marketing purposes.

5. Data Retention & Security

Your personal data is stored for as long as your CrimsonSMP account remains active. We implement appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, data loss, or disclosure.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & Data Protection)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion of your account and personal data.
  • The right to revoke access at any time by unlinking your Google Account.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or requests regarding your privacy, please contact us at: support@crimsonsmp.com.
