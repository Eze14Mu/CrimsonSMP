Server Rules

Please adhere to these guidelines to ensure a fair environment for everyone.

01. No Hacking or Unfair Advantages

The use of hacked clients, X-Ray texture packs, auto-clickers, or exploits is strictly forbidden. Any player caught using unfair advantages will face an immediate ban.

02. Respect All Players & Staff

Hate speech, toxicity, harassment, and excessive spamming in public chat will not be tolerated. Maintain a welcoming environment for all community members.

03. No Griefing or Stealing

Respect other players' builds and property. Unclaimed land griefing is subject to moderation review and rollback. Use land claims to safeguard your builds.
